How to play

  • Pick a ball from the bottom row of the depot with the crane (Space or click), carry it over a column and drop it (Space or click). Steer with ← → or the mouse.
  • Three or more balls of one colour side by side pop – together with every ball of that colour touching them. Five of a colour stacked fuse into one heavy ball.
  • Each column is a pan on a seesaw that shows its total weight. The heavier pan sinks and holds 8 balls, a raised pan only 6. When a seesaw swings over, the top ball of the rising pan is catapulted as many columns as the weight difference, towards the heavy side. The pan blinks and the path is shown before you drop.
  • A ball that flies off the board comes back on the other side – changed: balls become hearts, hearts become bombs, bombs become hearts.
  • Pop again while the bonus lamp is lit for double points. The game ends when a stack touches the ceiling.
