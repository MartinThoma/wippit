How to play

  • Balls drop one at a time. Steer with ← → or the mouse, drop with Space or a click.
  • Join three or more balls of one colour – side by side, stacked or diagonal – and they pop.
  • Every ball shows its weight. Each pair of columns rests on a seesaw. When one side becomes 3 or more heavier, it tips and the light side catapults its top ball into the next column.
  • Rainbow balls match any colour. Bombs blast everything around them when they land.
  • Pop balls to level up – the drops get faster. It's over when a column is full.
